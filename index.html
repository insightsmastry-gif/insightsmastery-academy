Why learners use these notes

Practical references you'll come back to

Every handbook is written by InsightsMastery instructors from real client work — the patterns, pitfalls and step-by-step fixes you need on the job.

Always up to date

Handbooks are revised as our instructors teach and new versions of Power BI ship, so what you read matches what you'll see on screen.

Printable PDF handbooks

Each note ships with a print-ready companion. Take it into a workshop, mark it up, hand it to a client — the layout is designed for paper, not for a scroll bar.

Reader mode with a live TOC

Notes open in a distraction-free reader: sticky table of contents, active-section highlighting and a progress bar so a 20-minute reference never feels endless.

Search and filters that keep up

Type to filter the library by title, topic or tag — results update as you type, entirely in the browser. Press / anywhere to jump into the search box.

Comfortable for long reading

Adjust the text size, switch on focus mode, or read in dark mode for late-night revision — the site remembers your choice. Toggle the theme with T.

Free, no sign-up

Read every handbook and download every PDF without an account, an email wall or a paywall — and without being tracked.
