About

Handbooks from the InsightsMastery classroom

The notes our instructors use to teach Power BI, DAX, Power Query and SQL Server — shared free so you can learn at your own pace, before, during or after a program.

Who writes these notes

Every handbook is written and maintained by InsightsMastery Academy instructors — practitioners who build Power BI models and SQL Server solutions for clients. The examples come from real reports, and each handbook is revised as we teach it and as the tools change.

Use them as a reference while you work, as revision for a class, or as a free way to see how we teach before you join a program. For hands-on practice with real datasets, try the Power BI projects.

Reading is private: there are no accounts, no analytics and no cookies beyond your theme preference, which stays in your own browser.
